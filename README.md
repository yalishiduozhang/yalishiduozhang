# 👋 Hi, I'm Xun Zhang (章勋)

**Undergraduate Student in Artificial Intelligence at Fuzhou University**

I am currently an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at **Fuzhou University**, and have been admitted to the **School of Computer Science and Engineering at Beihang University** for postgraduate study.

My research interests focus on large language model (LLM) inference optimization, AI infrastructure, and high-performance AI systems.

I am particularly interested in improving AI inference efficiency and computational resource utilization through algorithm design, inference strategy optimization, and systems engineering. I am also interested in system-level optimization for World Models and embodied AI.

- 🔬 **Research Interests:** LLM Inference Optimization, Efficient LLM Reasoning, AI Infrastructure.
- 🤖 **Related Areas:** World Models, Embodied AI, High-Performance Computing.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Efficient LLM Reasoning:** Optimizing reasoning processes, controlling reasoning paths, and reducing computational overhead.
- **AI Infrastructure:** High-performance computing, model inference systems, inference serving, and system performance optimization.
- **Embodied AI:** World Models, with a focus on inference deployment and system-level optimization.
